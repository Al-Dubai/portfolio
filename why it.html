Why ICT

I chose to study ICT because I want to gain the technical knowledge and practical skills needed to solve complex problems throughout my future career. Above all, I truly love the rewarding feeling of successfully solving a difficult problem. I have always been fascinated by how computers work and how technology drives our modern world. Interestingly, I previously studied at a medical university. However, during that time, I quickly realized my true passion lay elsewhere I enjoy fixing machines and systems much more than I enjoy fixing humans. To achieve my career goals, I am not only studying coding but also focusing heavily on computational logic and problem solving strategies. I am currently enrolled in the HBO ICT program and will soon be working in software development. Ultimately, as a software developer, I plan to build innovative applications that make people's lives significantly easier and provide them with a seamless digital experience.
